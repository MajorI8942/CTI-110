
# Baked Chicken

## Overview
- 1 Pack Of Chicken Thighs
- Salt
- Garlic Powder
- Creole Seasoning 
- Browning
- Sazon

## Instructions
1. Two tablespoons of each seasoning
2. Oil a pan and turn it on medium-high heat
3. Wait for the oil to get hot
4. Place chicken skin side down for 4 1/2 minutes then flip and cook for same time
5. Place chicken in oven at 375 for fifteen to twenty minutes

## Done When . . ?
- Chicken is white to the bone. Check with butterknife.
